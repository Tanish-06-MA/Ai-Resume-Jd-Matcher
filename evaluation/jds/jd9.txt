Job Title: Enterprise Sales Executive

Company: SalesForce Pro

Location: Mumbai, India

Experience: 3-6 Years

Job Description:

We are looking for a driven Enterprise Sales Executive to manage our B2B sales pipeline. You will identify new business opportunities, build client relationships, and close enterprise deals.

Responsibilities:
- Identify and qualify new enterprise sales opportunities
- Manage the full sales cycle from prospecting to closing
- Build and maintain relationships with C-level executives and decision makers
- Create compelling sales presentations and product demonstrations
- Negotiate contracts and pricing with enterprise clients
- Maintain accurate CRM records and sales forecasts in Salesforce
- Collaborate with marketing team on lead generation campaigns
- Achieve quarterly and annual revenue targets

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration or related field
- 3+ years of experience in B2B enterprise sales
- Proven track record of meeting or exceeding sales quotas
- Experience with CRM tools (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Strong presentation and negotiation skills
- Understanding of SaaS business models and pricing strategies
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills
- Willingness to travel for client meetings (30-40%)

Nice to Have:
- MBA degree
- Experience selling to Fortune 500 companies
- Knowledge of solution selling or SPIN selling methodologies
- Experience with international sales
