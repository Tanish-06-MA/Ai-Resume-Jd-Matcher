Job Title: Digital Marketing Manager

Company: BrandGrowth Solutions

Location: Mumbai, India

Experience: 4-7 Years

Job Description:

We are hiring a Digital Marketing Manager to lead our online marketing efforts. You will plan and execute digital campaigns across multiple channels to drive brand awareness, lead generation, and customer acquisition.

Responsibilities:
- Develop and execute comprehensive digital marketing strategies
- Manage SEO, SEM, and PPC campaigns across Google Ads and social media platforms
- Create and oversee content marketing initiatives including blog posts, whitepapers, and email campaigns
- Analyze marketing metrics and KPIs using Google Analytics and marketing automation tools
- Manage social media presence across LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter, and Facebook
- Plan and execute email marketing campaigns with segmentation and personalization
- Coordinate with creative teams for ad design and copywriting
- Manage marketing budgets and optimize ROI across channels

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, or related field
- 4+ years of experience in digital marketing
- Expertise in Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Facebook Ads Manager
- Strong knowledge of SEO best practices and tools (SEMrush, Ahrefs)
- Experience with marketing automation platforms (HubSpot, Mailchimp)
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Data-driven mindset with strong analytical abilities
- Experience with A/B testing and conversion rate optimization

Nice to Have:
- Google Ads and Analytics certifications
- Experience with influencer marketing
- Knowledge of marketing attribution models
- Video marketing and YouTube advertising experience
