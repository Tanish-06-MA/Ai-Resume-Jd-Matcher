Job Title: Senior Financial Analyst

Company: CapitalEdge Finance

Location: Delhi, India

Experience: 3-6 Years

Job Description:

We are looking for a Senior Financial Analyst to support our finance team in budgeting, forecasting, and financial reporting. You will analyze financial data, build financial models, and provide strategic recommendations to senior leadership.

Responsibilities:
- Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports and forecasts
- Build and maintain complex financial models for business planning
- Conduct variance analysis and identify key financial trends
- Support budgeting and planning processes across departments
- Perform DCF valuations and comparable company analysis
- Create executive dashboards and presentations for senior management
- Collaborate with accounting team on month-end close activities
- Evaluate investment opportunities and conduct due diligence

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or Economics
- 3+ years of experience in financial analysis or corporate finance
- Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and financial modeling
- Experience with ERP systems (SAP, Oracle Financials)
- Strong knowledge of GAAP and financial reporting standards
- Proficiency in data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI)
- CFA or CPA certification preferred
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

Nice to Have:
- MBA in Finance
- Experience with Python for financial analysis
- Knowledge of Bloomberg Terminal
- M&A or investment banking experience
