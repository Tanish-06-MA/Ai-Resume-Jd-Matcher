Job Title: Healthcare Administrator

Company: MedCare Hospitals

Location: Chennai, India

Experience: 5-8 Years

Job Description:

We are seeking an experienced Healthcare Administrator to oversee daily operations of our hospital facility. You will manage staff, ensure regulatory compliance, and optimize patient care delivery processes.

Responsibilities:
- Oversee daily hospital operations and administrative functions
- Manage departmental budgets and financial performance
- Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations (NABH, JCI standards)
- Develop and implement quality improvement programs
- Coordinate with medical staff on patient care protocols
- Manage electronic health records (EHR) systems
- Handle patient complaints and improve satisfaction scores
- Recruit, train, and evaluate administrative and support staff

Requirements:
- Master's degree in Hospital Administration (MHA) or Healthcare Management
- 5+ years of experience in hospital or healthcare administration
- Knowledge of healthcare regulations and accreditation standards
- Experience with hospital information systems (HIS) and EHR platforms
- Strong leadership and team management skills
- Understanding of medical billing and insurance processes
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
- Proficiency in healthcare analytics and reporting

Nice to Have:
- Experience with telemedicine operations
- Knowledge of healthcare supply chain management
- Certification in healthcare quality management
- Experience with Joint Commission International accreditation
