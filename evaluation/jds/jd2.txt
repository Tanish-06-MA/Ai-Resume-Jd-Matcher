Job Title: Data Scientist

Company: DataMinds Analytics

Location: Remote

Experience: 3-6 Years

Job Description:

We are seeking an experienced Data Scientist to join our analytics team. You will work on building machine learning models, analyzing large datasets, and delivering actionable insights to drive business decisions.

Responsibilities:
- Develop and deploy machine learning models for classification, regression, and clustering tasks
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) on large-scale datasets
- Build and maintain data pipelines using Python and SQL
- Create data visualizations and dashboards using Tableau or Power BI
- Collaborate with stakeholders to define business problems and translate them into data science solutions
- Conduct A/B testing and statistical analysis
- Implement deep learning models using TensorFlow or PyTorch
- Write research papers and present findings to technical and non-technical audiences

Requirements:
- Master's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or related field
- 3+ years of experience in data science or machine learning
- Strong proficiency in Python, NumPy, Pandas, and Scikit-learn
- Experience with deep learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch)
- Solid understanding of statistical modeling and hypothesis testing
- Experience with SQL and database management
- Knowledge of natural language processing (NLP) techniques
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS SageMaker, Google AI Platform)

Nice to Have:
- PhD in a quantitative field
- Experience with Apache Spark and big data technologies
- Knowledge of MLOps and model deployment pipelines
- Published research in machine learning conferences
